# Design Context

## Users

Friends, family, supporters, and the athlete checking one race from a phone at the venue or from home. They need an immediate answer to three questions: has Sylvain started, where was he last recorded, and what are his latest times and positions?

## Brand personality

Focused, kinetic, assured.

## Aesthetic direction

A calm, high-contrast race-day timing board built for outdoor daylight. The visual language borrows from lane markers and official result sheets: ink navy, water blue, signal orange, warm paper, condensed timing numerals, and strong horizontal rhythm. It should feel specific to an endurance event, not like a generic analytics dashboard.

## Typography

- Display and timing: Barlow Condensed.
- Interface and body: Atkinson Hyperlegible.

## Constraints

- Mobile first and readable in bright conditions.
- No authentication, API keys, or manually supplied event/race/bib IDs.
- Partial data is normal and must remain understandable.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- The page must retain the last good result during temporary network errors.

## Anti-references

- No generic white-card grid.
- No glossy gradients or gradient text.
- No decorative charting that implies continuous GPS tracking.
- No oversized marketing hero that pushes live timing below the fold.
